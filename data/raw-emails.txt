MAILMIND CUSTOMER SUPPORT EMAIL DATASET
======================================

Dataset contents:
- 40 online-shop customer emails
- 4 emails per each of 10 e-commerce intents
- 3 unrelated emails for out-of-domain retrieval/classification tests
- All emails addressed to support@mailmind.com
- Each email is separated by a clear divider

The "Intent" header is included as a dataset label. Remove it if you want to test
intent classification without exposing the ground-truth label to your model.
