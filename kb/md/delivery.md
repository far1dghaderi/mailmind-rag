# Northwind Goods Delivery Policy

## Delivery Requirements

* Orders with a total of $150 or more require a signature at delivery.

## Delivery Attempts

* Carriers make up to 3 delivery attempts on separate business days.
* If a signature is required and no one signs, the carrier leaves a notice instead of the parcel.

## Delivery Hours

* Carriers deliver only between 8 AM and 6 PM local time.

## Parcel Handling

* After the last failed attempt, the parcel is held at the carrier depot for 5 business days.
* A parcel that is not collected from the depot in that time is returned to our warehouse.
* Parcels can be left in a safe place only when no signature is required.
* A parcel left in a safe place is treated as delivered on the day it is dropped off.

## Redelivery and Returns

* A redelivery can be requested within 2 business days of a failed delivery attempt.
* A parcel returned to our warehouse as undeliverable is held for 21 days before it is restocked.
