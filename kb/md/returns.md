# Northwind Goods Returns Policy

## 1. Eligibility for Returns

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery. To be eligible for a return, the item must be unused and in its original packaging.

## 2. Refund Process

Refunds will be issued to the original payment method. Please note that refunds may take 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item.

## 3. Return Shipping

The customer is responsible for return shipping, unless the item is damaged or wrong. If the item is damaged or wrong, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo.

## 4. Exchanges

We allow exchanges for size or color, subject to stock availability. Please note that sale items are final sale and cannot be returned.

## 5. Refunds on Discounted Orders

Refunds on discounted orders will be for the amount actually paid, not the original price.

## 6. Damaged or Wrong Items

If you receive a damaged or wrong item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo. We will work with you to resolve the issue.

## 7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about our returns policy, please don't hesitate to contact us. We're here to help.
