# Northwind Goods Shipping Policy

## Shipping Areas
We ship to the United States and Canada only.

## Shipping Options
We offer two shipping options:

1. **Standard Shipping**
	* Takes 3-5 business days
	* Costs $4.99 (free on orders over $50)
2. **Express Shipping**
	* Takes 1-2 business days
	* Costs $12.99

## Shipping Schedule
Orders placed before 2 PM ship the same business day.

## Tracking Your Order
A tracking number is emailed to you once your order ships.

## Address Changes
You can change your shipping address within 2 hours of placing your order.

## Lost Packages
If a package has not arrived 10 days after the estimated delivery date, please contact us to start a lost package claim.

## Shipping Restrictions
We cannot ship to PO boxes.
