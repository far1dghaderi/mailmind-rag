# Northwind Goods Gift Card Policy

## Buying a Gift Card

You can purchase a gift card from Northwind Goods in any whole-dollar amount from $10 to $500.

## Receiving Your Gift Card Code

After purchasing a gift card, the code will be sent to the email address you entered at purchase. This will happen within 15 minutes of the purchase.

## Using Your Gift Card

You can use your gift card to pay for merchandise on the Northwind Goods website. You can spend your gift card balance across several orders until it reaches zero.

## Gift Card Details

* A gift card code is 16 characters long and is not case-sensitive.
* A gift card has no expiry date.
* Unused gift card balances are not paid out in cash.

## Resending a Lost Gift Card Code

If you lose your gift card code, you can have it resent to your email address up to 3 times.

## Storing Your Gift Card Codes

You can store up to 10 gift card codes in your Northwind Goods customer account at once.
