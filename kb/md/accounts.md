**Northwind Goods Accounts Policy**

1. **Creating an Account**
	* To create an account, you will need to provide an email address and a password.
	* Your password must be at least 8 characters long.
2. **Checking Out as a Guest**
	* You can also check out as a guest without creating an account.
	* Guest orders will not appear in your account's order history.
3. **Order History**
	* Your order history will show orders placed in the past 2 years.
4. **Email Preferences**
	* You can turn off marketing emails at any time in your account settings.
	* Even if you turn off marketing emails, you will still receive order and delivery emails.
5. **Account Deletion**
	* If you request to delete your account, we will confirm your request by email within 1 business day.
	* Deleting your account will remove your personal data within 45 days.
	* However, we will keep a record of your orders for 7 years for accounting purposes.
6. **Password Reset**
	* A password reset link is valid for 60 minutes.
