**Northwind Goods Pre-Order Policy**
=====================================

**1. Pre-Order Basics**
------------------------

A pre-order lets a customer buy an item before its release date. Pre-order items are charged when they ship, not when the order is placed.

**2. Pre-Order Limits**
------------------------

A customer can pre-order up to 2 units of the same item.

**3. Release Date Changes**
---------------------------

A pre-order release date can move by up to 60 days after the date first shown. Pre-order customers are emailed each time the release date changes.

**4. Item Substitution**
-------------------------

A pre-order can be switched to an in-stock item of the same or lower price before it ships.

**5. Shipping and Delivery**
---------------------------

Pre-order customers receive a release email 3 days before the item ships.

**6. Returns**
----------------

A pre-ordered item can be returned only after it has been delivered.

**7. Backorders**
------------------

A backorder is an item that is out of stock and expected back in 1 to 6 weeks. If a backordered item is still out of stock after 30 days, a new estimated date will be shown.
