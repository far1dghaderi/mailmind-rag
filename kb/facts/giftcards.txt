Store name: Northwind Goods
Gift cards can be bought in any whole-dollar amount from $10 to $500
The gift card code is sent by email to the address entered at purchase
Gift card emails are sent within 15 minutes of the purchase
A gift card code is 16 characters long and is not case-sensitive
A gift card can be used to pay for merchandise on the Northwind Goods website
A gift card can be spent across several orders until its balance reaches zero
A gift card has no expiry date
Unused gift card balances are not paid out in cash
A lost gift card code can be resent to the purchaser's email address up to 3 times
A customer account can hold up to 10 gift card codes at once
